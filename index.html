How we work

Five stages. No surprises.

  1. Discover

    Goals, users and budget, turned into a written scope and fixed quote.

  2. Design

    Wireframes, then polished screens or slides you approve first.

  3. Build

    Two-week sprints, a live staging link and weekly demos.

  4. Launch

    Testing, deployment, store submissions and handover.

  5. Care

    A maintenance agreement keeps it secure and improving.
