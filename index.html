How a project runs

Five stages, no surprises.

  1. Discover

    Workshops on goals, users and budget. You get a written scope and fixed quote.

  2. Design

    Wireframes, then high-fidelity screens or slides you approve before code starts.

  3. Build

    Two-week sprints with a live staging link and a weekly demo.

  4. Launch

    Testing, deployment, store submissions and team handover.

  5. Care

    A maintenance agreement keeps it secure, updated and improving.
